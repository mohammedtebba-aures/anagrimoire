## Revisit when

The condition that should make a future you reopen this. Often a version
number, a scale threshold, or a failed experiment.

---

**When to write an ADR:** the choice is expensive to reverse, or you will not
remember the reasoning in three months, or you had to think for more than ten
minutes. Not for things like picking a variable name.

**Never delete an ADR.** Supersede it with a new one and mark the old status.
The history of how the thinking changed is the valuable part.
